# Privacy Policy — Mind Silence

Last updated: 3 September 2026

Mind Silence (“the app”) is a meditation training game published under the package name `com.mindsilence.game`.

## Data we collect

We do **not** collect, transmit, or share personal data.

The app does not use accounts, the internet, advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or third-party SDKs that process personal information.

## Data stored on your device

Session progress stays **only on your device**, in local app storage:

- calendar date of a training day
- number of attempts
- total time spent
- best level reached

This information is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it. Cloud backup is disabled.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
